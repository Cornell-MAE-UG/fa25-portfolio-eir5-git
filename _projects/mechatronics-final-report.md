## Project Overview

The mechantronics final project was focused about creating a robot that would compete against another robot. The competition would focused about the robots sterting on opposite ends of the board and would approach the center at the end of the countdown. The center of the board would contain small cubes of which each of the robots would try to collect the most amount. At the end of this collection sweep, blocks would be counted, and the robot with the most amount of block collected would be claimed winner.

Within the final project, I handled the design and building aspect for the robot. One of my team members focused on the coding, while I would try to integrate the design and movement of the robot of itself to be considered in the coding.
